Build your About Me document

0 / 4 done

NotebookLM only ever writes from what you feed it, which is exactly why it's the right first stop: it turns scattered documents into one grounded, first-person draft you can trust.

Go to notebooklm.google.com and sign in with your Google account. It's free — the free plan includes up to 100 notebooks and 50 sources per notebook, which is far more than this exercise needs.
Click Create new and name it "Personal Knowledge Base."
Add as many as you have:
  • Your existing CV or resume
  • Your LinkedIn profile text (paste it directly)
  • A portfolio or project summary
  • A short professional bio or past job descriptions
Paste this prompt into the NotebookLM chat box, then copy the result into Word or Docs and save as About_Me.docx.
Prompt
Create a comprehensive professional About Me document based solely on the uploaded information. Include the following sections:

- Personal introduction
- Core skills and technical competencies
- Professional experience (with roles, organisations, and dates)
- Key achievements and measurable outcomes
- Education and qualifications
- Certifications and training
- Notable projects
- Interests and professional aspirations

Write in a warm, professional first-person voice. Do not invent or add any information not present in the uploaded sources.

Try the Audio Overview

NotebookLM can turn your sources into a short spoken discussion — a fast, painless way to hear your own career summarised back to you.

Mind what you upload

Files are processed on Google's servers. Stick to career material — leave out anything sensitive you wouldn't want stored in the cloud.
