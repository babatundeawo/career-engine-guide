Stay current

Keep your knowledge base alive

Your documents are only as useful as they are up to date. Re-upload whenever life moves — a stale knowledge base produces stale documents.

Life eventFile to update
New job or roleMaster_CV.docx
New qualification or certificationBoth files
New project or achievementMaster_CV.docx
Change in career directionAbout_Me.docx
New skill acquiredMaster_CV.docx
Published work or awardBoth files

Re-upload, don't append

When you update a file, replace it in your Claude Project's knowledge rather than adding a second, older version alongside it.

Set a quarterly reminder

Even with no major life event, a 15-minute quarterly review catches small wins you'd otherwise forget to log.
