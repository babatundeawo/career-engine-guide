Phase 2 · Word or Docs

Build your Master CV

0 / 4 done

This is your complete, unedited career record — not a targeted CV. Length doesn't matter here. Completeness does.

Start fresh in Microsoft Word or Google Docs.
  • Full name & contact information
  • Professional summary
  • Core skills
  • Work experience (every role, with dates)
  • Education & qualifications
  • Certifications & training
  • Projects & achievements
  • Publications and volunteer work (if any)
Include everything. Claude will choose what's relevant later — your job right now is just to get it all down.
Save it as Master_CV.docx. You now have both files ready.

Dates over descriptions

Precise start/end dates for every role matter more than polished prose here — Claude can rewrite the prose later, but it can't invent a date.

Quantify where you can

Numbers — team size, budget, percentage improvement — give every future document something concrete to point to.
