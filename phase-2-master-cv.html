This is your complete, unedited career record — not a targeted CV. Length doesn't matter here. Completeness does.

📅

Dates over descriptions

Precise start/end dates for every role matter more than polished prose here — Claude can rewrite the prose later, but it can't invent a date.

🧮

Quantify where you can

Numbers — team size, budget, percentage improvement — give every future document something concrete to point to.
