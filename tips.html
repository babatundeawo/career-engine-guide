Pro tips

Get sharper results

Small habits that make every document Claude generates a little more precise.

📎

Always paste the full job description

The more context Claude has, the more precisely it can align your real experience to what the role actually needs.

🚩

Treat gaps as a to-do list

If Claude flags a missing detail, add it to your Master CV and re-upload before regenerating.

🎧

Try NotebookLM's Audio Overview

Hearing your own career summarised out loud is great interview prep — it helps you tell your story before you're asked to.

🗂️

Keep a dated folder

Store generated documents in a folder like Career Documents — 2026 so nothing gets lost or overwritten.

♾️

This isn't just for job hunting

The same knowledge base writes grant applications, speaker bios, consulting profiles, and personal website copy.

🧵

Ask for options, not just one draft

Request two or three tonal variations of a cover letter opener and pick the one that actually sounds like you.
